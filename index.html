VOICE CHAT - PLEASE READ

  • Voice works only with friends in the same room. Your browser will ask for microphone access.
  • Audio goes directly between the players' browsers (or via a relay server). It is never recorded or stored by this game.
  • Friends in a call can technically see your network address (IP), like in any peer-to-peer call.
  • You can mute your microphone ([V]), mute any player, or leave voice at any time. Text chat is always available instead.
  • Never share personal data. Be kind to each other.
